New exposure limits start 1 December. Is your extraction ready?

From 1 December 2026, Workplace Exposure Limits (WELs) replace Australia's Workplace Exposure Standards. Most limits are unchanged, but some have been reduced, and welding fume is already held to 1 mg/m³, down from 5 mg/m³.

Employers must continue to ensure workers are not exposed above the applicable limit and to eliminate or minimise exposure so far as is reasonably practicable.

Extraction is part of your risk-control strategy

Effective local exhaust ventilation, appropriate filtration and ongoing maintenance all contribute to a broader workplace risk-control strategy. Inspection and maintenance help identify issues that may be compromising extraction effectiveness before they become a safety or compliance concern.
